# Sunburst Sanctuary LLC — Robert's Contribution Agreement
## Draft — For Discussion Before Formalizing

### Between
Sunburst Sanctuary LLC ("Company") and Robert ("Contributor")

### Robert's Role: Tech Lead & Service Delivery

Robert agrees to provide the following services to the Company:
1. In-home technology support visits for elderly clients in Santa Fe, NM and surrounding areas
2. Device setup, configuration, and troubleshooting
3. Scam awareness and digital safety training
4. Client education and training
5. Technical documentation and visit logs
6. Assistance with service delivery logistics (scheduling, routing, supplies)

### Time Commitment
- **Phase 0 (Trial):** 5-10 hours/week, flexible
- **Phase 1 (Formal):** 10-20 hours/week, with scheduled client visits
- Robert sets his own schedule within client availability windows
- No minimum hours required during trial phase

### Compensation & Equity
- **Trial phase (Months 1-6):** No equity. Robert operates as an independent contractor, paid per visit ($25-35/visit or 50% of visit revenue, whichever is higher)
- **After trial (Month 6+):** 25% equity vesting begins (see Operating Agreement Article 3)
- **Ongoing:** Profit distributions proportional to ownership (25% once fully vested)
- **Expenses:** Company reimburses mileage, supplies, and equipment costs

### What Robert Is NOT Responsible For
- Business registration, taxes, or legal compliance (Adora's role)
- Marketing, website, or digital presence (Narusya's role)
- Client billing or financial management (Adora's role)
- Hiring or managing Monika (Adora's role)

### Robert's Commitments
1. Perform all services to the best of his ability
2. Maintain visit logs for every client interaction
3. Follow scope-of-work boundaries (no handling money, no remote access without consent)
4. Carry general liability insurance (company reimburses)
5. Communicate openly about capacity, concerns, and ideas
6. Participate in quarterly business reviews

### Company's Commitments to Robert
1. Provide training on elderly-specific tech support (scam patterns, communication style, etc.)
2. Provide all necessary tools, supplies, and branded materials
3. Handle all business administration (billing, scheduling, insurance, taxes)
4. Reimburse approved expenses within 14 days
5. Provide a clear path to full equity ownership
6. Not pressure Robert into commitments he's not comfortable with

### Trial Period & Exit
- **Trial period:** 6 months
- **Either party may exit during trial** with 30 days notice
- **No equity forfeiture** during trial (there's no equity yet)
- **After trial:** Robert's equity begins vesting; standard exit terms apply (Operating Agreement Article 6)
- **If Robert exits during vesting:** Unvested equity returns to company; vested equity subject to buyout

### Robert's Concerns — Addressed

**"I don't believe in my tech support skills"**
- Business-level tech support is more rigorous than consumer support
- The skills transfer down, not up
- Trial period exists specifically to build confidence with data
- No one expects perfection — elderly clients need patience and clarity, not enterprise SLAs

**"I'm worried about making mistakes"**
- Scope-of-work template limits liability
- General liability insurance covers accidents
- Visit logs document everything
- Small market = small mistakes = small consequences
- Mistakes are expected and recoverable

**"I can't bet on myself"**
- The trial period means you're not betting blind
- You're betting on 6 months of real data
- If it doesn't work, you walk away with experience and no equity obligation
- The 25% is earned, not given
