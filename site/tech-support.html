Tech Support for Seniors

Patient. Respectful. No jargon. No rushing.

We come to your home, sit with you, and help you feel confident using your devices. Whether it's setting up a new phone, learning to video call your grandkids, or making sure you don't click on a scam — we're here to help. No rush. No judgment. We explain everything in plain language.

Service area: Santa Fe, NM and Brownfield, TX (Terry County). We come to you — no driving to a store, no waiting on hold. In-home visits at your pace.

What We Do

Password & Security Setup — We set up a password manager (Bitwarden — free and secure), migrate your existing passwords, enable two-factor authentication on your important accounts, and teach you how to use it all. This is the single most important thing you can do to protect yourself online. (60-90 minutes)
Tech Orientation Visit — We sit with you, look at all your devices, and figure out what's working and what isn't. We create a personalized cheat sheet you can keep by your side. (90 minutes)
Device Setup & Configuration — New phone, tablet, or computer? We'll set it up, transfer your data, configure everything, and teach you how to use it. (60 minutes)
Scam & Safety Check — We review your devices for suspicious apps, emails, and links. We set up basic protections and create a personalized scam recognition guide. (45 minutes)
Monthly Check-In — A regular visit to make sure everything still works, answer new questions, update software, and check for new concerns. (30-45 minutes)
Emergency Phone/Video Support — Something urgent? We're available by phone or video call within 24 hours. (30 minutes)

How a Visit Works

1. You reach out. Email us with what you need. We ask a few questions and schedule a time that works for you — usually within a few days.
2. We come to you. No driving, no waiting on hold. We sit with you at your kitchen table or wherever you're comfortable, look at your devices, and explain everything in plain language.
3. You leave confident. You get a personalized cheat sheet to keep by your side, and we're a phone call or email away if something comes up later.

What We Don't Do

We're technology helpers, not financial advisors. We never handle money, manage bank accounts, or make purchases on your behalf. We never remote-access your devices without your explicit consent during a session. We explain everything before we do it, and you're always in control.

Common Questions

"I use the same password for everything. Is that bad?"
Yes — if one account gets hacked, they all do. We set up a password manager that remembers unique passwords for every account, so you only need to remember one. We also enable two-factor authentication, which adds a second layer of protection even if a password is stolen.
"I'm not good with technology. Will you be patient?"
Absolutely. That's the whole point. We never rush, never use jargon, and never make you feel bad for asking questions. If something doesn't make sense, we explain it differently until it does.
"I've been scammed before. Can you help?"
Yes. We'll review your devices, set up protections, and teach you how to recognize and avoid scams. We also create a personalized guide you can keep by your phone or computer.
"I don't have a computer, just a phone. Can you still help?"
Yes! Most of our clients just have a phone or tablet. We help with whatever you have.
"My grandkids set everything up but I don't know how to use it."
That's our most common situation. We'll sit with you and teach you how to use what you already have — at your pace, in your words.
