Who we are

Coil and Code is a two-person shop. We build small, honest command-line tools and do real work for real people. No subscriptions, no clouds, no accounts. Just Python and your files. Your data never leaves your machine.

What we believe

  • Zero-dependency. Python 3.8+ stdlib only. No venv drama, no pip install nightmares.
  • Tested. Real test cases against real edge inputs before anything is listed.
  • Open. The full source lives in our repo. You can read exactly what you're buying before you buy it.
  • Honest. Fixed price, no surprises. We define "done" precisely before we start.
  • Clean money only. We refuse AI art that undercuts human artists, political or educational prose, harmful or deceptive work, and promises we can't verify.
