Work With Us

Sunburst Sanctuary is growing. We're looking for patient, kind, tech-savvy people who want to help seniors feel confident with their devices. If you believe technology should make life easier — not harder — we want to hear from you.

Open Roles

Technology Support Technician

Santa Fe, NM Part-time / Contract

Visit seniors in their homes. Help them with phones, tablets, computers, WiFi, passwords, and scams. You're not just fixing things — you're teaching. Patience is the job.

What You'll Do

  • In-home tech support visits (60-90 minutes)
  • Set up password managers and 2FA
  • Teach device basics in plain language
  • Scam awareness training
  • Document visits and recommendations
  • Follow up with clients as needed

Requirements

  • Comfortable with phones, tablets, computers, and WiFi
  • Patient and respectful with older adults
  • Reliable transportation
  • Proof of current flu and COVID-19 vaccination
  • Background check (we'll handle this)
  • Must be willing to wear a mask upon client request

Nice to Have

  • Experience teaching or helping non-technical people
  • Familiarity with Bitwarden or other password managers
  • Bilingual (English/Spanish)
  • Flexible schedule

Secretary / Office Coordinator

Remote / Santa Fe, NM Part-time

Handle scheduling, client intake, paperwork, and coordination. The glue that holds everything together. You'll be the first point of contact for clients and technicians.

What You'll Do

  • Schedule and coordinate appointments
  • Process client intake forms
  • Query and record client preferences (masking, health considerations)
  • Verify technician vaccination records
  • Handle billing and invoicing (Stripe)
  • Maintain client and visit records

Requirements

  • Organized and detail-oriented
  • Comfortable with email, spreadsheets, and basic software
  • Warm, professional phone manner
  • Reliable internet connection

Remote Technology Consultant

Remote Contract

Provide remote technology consulting and support for Sunburst Sanctuary clients and operations. Handle device troubleshooting, software configuration, security setup, and technical documentation from anywhere. No in-home visits required.

What You'll Do

  • Remote tech support via phone, email, and video call
  • Device setup and configuration guidance
  • Password manager and 2FA setup assistance
  • Scam awareness and prevention support
  • Technical documentation and client follow-up
  • Research and recommend tools and solutions

Requirements

  • Strong technical skills across phones, tablets, computers, and networks
  • Excellent communication skills (phone and written)
  • Self-motivated and able to work independently
  • Reliable internet connection
  • Proof of current flu and COVID-19 vaccination

Nice to Have

  • Experience with Bitwarden or other password managers
  • Familiarity with remote support tools
  • Background in IT or tech support
  • Bilingual (English/Spanish)

Why Work With Us?

  • Meaningful work. You're not just fixing computers. You're helping people stay connected to their families, their communities, and their independence.
  • Flexible schedule. Part-time and contract positions. Set your own availability.
  • Fair pay. Competitive rates for your area. We believe good work deserves good pay.
  • Training provided. We'll teach you our methods, our tools, and our approach.
  • No degree required. What matters is patience, kindness, and a willingness to learn.
