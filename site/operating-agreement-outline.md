### Article 3: Equity Vesting (Robert)

Robert's 25% equity vests as follows:
- **0-6 months:** 0% (cliff period — trial phase)
- **Month 6:** 25% of Robert's share vests (6.25% of total company)
- **Months 7-18:** Remaining 75% of Robert's share vests monthly (1.39% of total per month)
- **Full vesting:** 18 months from agreement date

If Robert leaves before full vesting, unvested equity returns to the company. Vested equity is subject to buyout terms (Article 6).

**Adora and Narusya's equity is fully vested immediately.**

### Article 4: Management & Voting

- **Management:** Member-managed (all members participate in decisions)
- **Voting:** By ownership percentage (Adora 25%, Robert 25%, Narusya 50%)
- **Major decisions** (require unanimous consent):
  - Adding new members
  - Selling the company or major assets
  - Changing the operating agreement
  - Taking on debt over $5,000
  - Changing the company name or brand
- **Day-to-day decisions:** Majority vote (Narusya's 50% is decisive, but consensus is preferred)
- **Deadlock resolution:** If a 50/50 split occurs, an independent mediator is consulted

### Article 5: Profit Distribution

- Profits distributed **annually** (or more frequently by unanimous consent)
- Distribution proportional to ownership (25/25/50)
- **Reinvestment first:** Members may vote to retain earnings for growth (insurance, equipment, marketing)
- No member may unilaterally withdraw funds
- Losses allocated proportionally

### Article 6: Exit & Buyout

**If a member leaves voluntarily:**
- Vested equity is subject to company buyout at fair market value
- Valuation method: 2x annual revenue (or agreed-upon method)
- Payment over 12-24 months (not lump sum)
- Non-compete: 12 months within 50 miles of Santa Fe, NM

**If a member is removed for cause:**
- Unvested equity forfeited immediately
- Vested equity bought out at 50% of fair market value
- "Cause" defined as: fraud, criminal conviction, breach of fiduciary duty, or failure to perform duties for 90+ days

**If a member dies or becomes incapacitated:**
- Equity transfers to estate/heirs
- Company has right of first refusal to buy back
- 12-month buyout period

- Growth focus: Santa Fe NM and adjacent NM communities (Los Alamos, Española, Taos, Albuquerque metro as capacity allows)
